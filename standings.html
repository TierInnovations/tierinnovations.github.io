Every lifter, in their band

The board is the ladder itself: nine colour bands from IX at the top to I at the bottom, each showing that tier’s thresholds for men and women. Each lifter sits in the band they’ve earned, with their bench, squat and deadlift beside their name.

Friends, your gym, your city

Switch the board with one tap. Friends shows the people you train with. Gym shows everyone at your gym. City shows every lifter in your city.

Didn’t add a gym or city when you signed up? Set them up right from the board.

Your place, in plain numbers

Your tier screen tells you where you rank in your tier, like 2nd of 12, and exactly how many pounds stand between you and the next one.

Inside a band, lifters are ordered by their bench, squat and deadlift added together, strongest first.

Two ladders

Men and women are ranked on their own ladders, so every tier is reachable. The colours are shared: a Tier VI is Fire Red for everyone.

Unranked is a starting line

Until all three lifts clear Tier I, you’re unranked, shown below the ladder, and not counted against anyone’s place. A lift you haven’t entered shows as not entered, never as zero.

Just train: every set you log records your best, and you move onto the board by yourself.

See the whole ladder

Every threshold for every tier, for men and women, is on the Tiers page. Try the calculator to see where you’d land.
